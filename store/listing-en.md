## Name

YouTube Gamepad

## Short description

Browse YouTube with a gamepad, control playback, and search with an on-screen keyboard supporting English and Chinese Pinyin.

## Detailed description

Connect a controller to your computer to browse videos, control playback, and search on YouTube. YouTube Gamepad brings common actions to your D-pad, analog stick, and buttons, with a large-screen interface designed for controller use.

Features

- Browse videos with the D-pad or left stick, select a video, and go back through menus.
- Play or pause, seek backward or forward, adjust volume, and access captions through the quick menu.
- Use a TV-style player interface and recommendation cards. Automatic browser fullscreen can be turned off in settings.
- Search with an on-screen keyboard for English, numbers, symbols, and offline Chinese Pinyin candidates. Reuse your five most recent searches made through the extension.
- Customize controller shortcuts, stick deadzone, and directional repeat timing.
- See Xbox, PlayStation, or Switch-style button hints based on the controller reported by your browser. Compatibility depends on the device and the browser's standard gamepad mapping.

Getting started

1. Install the extension and open or refresh www.youtube.com.
2. Connect a controller with a browser-supported standard mapping. Click the page and press a controller button.
3. Use the D-pad or left stick to move, the confirm button to select, and Start to open the quick menu. Open full settings from the extension's toolbar popup.

The original YouTube interface remains available when no compatible controller is connected or controller operation is disabled. The extension interface is in English. The on-screen keyboard supports English and Chinese Pinyin input.

Data and privacy

The extension processes controls and preferences locally. It does not automatically upload usage records or diagnostics to the developer and includes no advertising analytics or tracking SDK. It reads relevant content on the current YouTube page to provide navigation, playback controls, and recommendations. Search text is synchronized with YouTube's search field, and submitted searches are processed by YouTube. Thumbnails and other page resources may load from YouTube's resource servers.

Recent searches, keyboard language, and learned Pinyin rankings are stored in YouTube's local website storage, which is accessible to scripts on that website. Settings and size-limited error records use extension-local storage. Diagnostic files are saved locally only when you choose to export them. See the privacy policy for details and deletion instructions.

YouTube Gamepad is an independently developed extension and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google. It is intended for the desktop website at www.youtube.com. Changes to YouTube may affect some features.
