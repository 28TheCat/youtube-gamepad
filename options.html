How to use gamepad controls

Turning off gamepad controls restores the YouTube interface.

During playback, press Start to open the quick menu to search, toggle captions, adjust volume, or open all settings.

Display & layout

Make YouTube easier to use from a distance. Enable either option independently.

Use a TV-style header and playback controls when a gamepad is detected. The sidebar and video lists keep their YouTube layout.

Make the browser window fullscreen to hide its tabs and address bar.

TV layout and fullscreen explained

TV layout changes the YouTube page. Automatic fullscreen expands the browser window containing the current YouTube tab, like pressing F11.

Disconnecting the gamepad, turning off automatic fullscreen, or disabling the extension restores the previous window state. Fullscreen entered manually stays active.

The page-filling video layout is also independent of browser fullscreen.

Connection and controls

Connect a standard-layout gamepad, click the YouTube page, then press a gamepad button.

D-pad / left stick: Select. A Confirm, B Back, Start Quick menu.

Gamepad not responding?

Check that your gamepad is connected and gamepad controls are enabled under General. Return to YouTube, click the page, then press a gamepad button.

If the issue persists, export the local diagnostic records below to help troubleshoot.

Local diagnostics

Keeps recent errors locally, up to 100 records and 32,768 characters. Older records are removed when these limits are reached. Export them to review or share.

Records include the time, extension version, module, error, stack trace, and interface state. Video titles, comments, and full page addresses are excluded. URLs in error text are hidden.
